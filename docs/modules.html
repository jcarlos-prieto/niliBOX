This is the list of modules available at the moment. This list can be extended in the future.

Remember that you can run any module locally on your computer or from another niliBOX installation available in your network or via Internet.

If you are interested on creating your own modules, check the Development Reference.

Radio
Icom IC-PCR100
  • Control of an Icom IC-PCR100 receiver via serial port (local or remote)
  • Includes the main functionalities of the receiver
  • 1 million station memories in 1000 banks
  • Visualization of bandscope and band spectrogram
  • Visualization of audio in time, frequency and spectrogram
  • Filtering of audio output
  • TSQL, scan and audio recording functions
Icom IC-PCR1000
  • Control of an Icom IC-PCR1000 receiver via serial port (local or remote)
  • Includes the main functionalities of the receiver
  • 1 million station memories in 1000 banks
  • Visualization of bandscope and band spectrogram
  • Visualization of audio in time, frequency and spectrogram
  • Filtering of audio output
  • TSQL, scan and audio recording functions
Icom IC-PCR1500
  • Control of an Icom IC-PCR1500 or IC-R1500 receiver via USB port (local or remote)
  • Includes the main functionalities of the receiver
  • 1 million station memories in 1000 banks
  • Visualization of bandscope and band spectrogram
  • Visualization of audio in time, frequency and spectrogram
  • Filtering of audio output
  • DSP, TSQL, DTCS, scan and audio recording functions
SDRplay RSP1A
  • Control of a SDRplay SDR radio receiver RSP1A. Other models partially supported
  • Sampling frequency up to 12MHz
  • Continuous tuning from 1kHZ to 2GHz
  • RSP AGC and audio AGC functions
  • Other RSP functionalities: bandwidth control, Bias-T, etc.
  • 1 million station memories in 1000 banks
  • Visualization of bandscope and band spectrogram
  • Visualization of audio in time, frequency and spectrogram
  • Filtering of audio output
  • Deemphasis filter, noise reduction, and record functions (audio and raw)
  • No SDRplay drivers needed
RTL2832U SDR
  • Control of a RTL2832U SDR dongle (local or remote)
  • Compatible with Rafael Micro, Elonics E4000 and Fitipower tuners
  • Control of sampling frequency, IF filter and RF gain
  • RTL AGC and audio AGC functions
  • 1 million station memories in 1000 banks
  • Visualization of bandscope and band spectrogram
  • Visualization of audio in time, frequency and spectrogram
  • Filtering of audio output
  • Deemphasis filter, noise reduction, and record functions (audio and raw)
Digital modes
  • Decodes most digital modes from audio source
  • Can be chained to another receiver running in niliBOX as audio source
  • Compatible with AMBE3000 devices and sofwtare emulation (testing purposes only)
  • Filtering of audio output
  • Audio recording function
Audio
Audio Player
  • Basic audio player of wav files
  • Usuful to send audio output to other modules
  • Filtering of audio output
Remote audio
  • Transfer of audio from remote to local and vice versa
  • Filtering of audio output
Video
Webcam
  • Visualization of webcam devices (local or remote)
  • Screenshot and recording functions
Automation
Relays
  • Control of relays connected via USB or serial port (local or remote)
  • Big flexibility to compose commands for ON and OFF
  • Capable to read relay status if the functionality is available on the device
Test
Driver test
  • Module to test hardware capabilities
  • Hardware scanning
  • Test of graphics
  • Test of audio
  • Test of communications
